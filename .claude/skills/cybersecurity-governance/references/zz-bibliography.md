# Bibliography and further reading

The following works were consulted in preparing this edition or are recommended for readers who want to go further. They are not cited directly in the text.

Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission. (2017). *Enterprise risk management — Integrating with strategy and performance*. COSO.

De Haes, S., Van Grembergen, W., & Debreceny, R. S. (2013). COBIT 5 and enterprise governance of information technology: Building blocks and research opportunities. *Journal of Information Systems, 27*(1), 307–324. https://doi.org/10.2308/isys-50422

Dupont, B. (2024). The governance of cybercrime: An ecological approach. *Canadian Journal of Criminology and Criminal Justice, 66*(2), 1–23. https://doi.org/10.3138/cjccj-2024-0034

Institute of Internal Auditors. (2024). *Global internal audit standards*. https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/global-internal-audit-standards/

Institute of Internal Auditors. (2025). *Cybersecurity topical requirement* \[and user guide\]. https://www.theiia.org/

Institute of Internal Auditors. (2025). *Third-party topical requirement* \[and user guide\]. https://www.theiia.org/

International Organization for Standardization & International Electrotechnical Commission. (2019). *Information technology — Security techniques — Guidelines for the assessment of information security controls* (ISO/IEC TS 27008:2019). https://www.iso.org/standard/67397.html

International Organization for Standardization & International Electrotechnical Commission. (2020). *Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Guidelines for information security management systems auditing* (ISO/IEC Standard No. 27007:2020). https://www.iso.org/standard/77802.html

International Organization for Standardization. (2018). *Guidelines for auditing management systems* (ISO Standard No. 19011:2018). https://www.iso.org/standard/70017.html

International Organization for Standardization. (2025). *Compliance management systems — Guidance for the evaluation of the effectiveness of compliance management systems* (ISO Standard No. 37302:2025). https://www.iso.org/

ISACA. (2013). *COBIT 5 for risk*. ISACA.

ISACA. (2026). *IT audit framework (ITAF): A professional practices framework for IT audit* (5th ed.). ISACA.

Léger, M.-A. (2021). *Implementing information technology management with an ontology of cybersecurity professional skills* \[Doctoral thesis, Université du Québec en Outaouais\]. https://di.uqo.ca/id/eprint/1497/

Léger, M.-A. (2024). *Introduction to cybersecurity governance for business technology management: A guide for students* (Version 1.1i) \[Course text\]. Concordia University.

Léger, M.-A. (n.d.). *Introduction to strategic information risk management* \[Unpublished manuscript\].
