# CyberRiskGuardian Education Package 1.0

*Version française : `LISEZMOI.md`*

Teaching materials for instructors and students who use CyberRiskGuardian in a course on cybersecurity risk management, governance, IT audit or business technology management. Every document exists in English (`en/`) and French (`fr/`), as Markdown sources and as finished Word and PDF files.

## Contents

| Document | For | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| `01_Instructor_Guide` | Instructors, TAs | The method in one page, learning outcomes, tool tracks, four integration models (one workshop to a full term), set-up checklist, lab facilitation notes, the hard concepts to teach, generative AI policy, troubleshooting |
| `02_Student_Lab_Workbook` | Students | Twelve step-by-step labs on the MediBec teaching case, formula sheet, templates, self-check answers, glossary |
| `03_Assessment_Kit` | Students and instructors | 0–10 grading scale, briefs and rubrics for the risk assessment (A1), IAM policy (A2) and audit plan (A3), four shorter formats, self-checks, peer-review form, AI acknowledgement |
| `04_Case_Library` | Students and instructors | Five fictional business cases and how to choose one; the student versions of the cases are in `cases/` |
| `slides/CRG_Intro_Deck` | Instructors | An introductory class on the CRG method (PowerPoint) |
| `templates/CyberRiskGuardian_Excel_Model_Blank_v2-4e.xlsx` | Students and instructors | The blank CyberRiskGuardian Excel model (Analyse, S1–S10, M1–M10) used for the risk-assessment assignment — free |

## Educator Edition

Answer keys, a graded quiz bank, rubric calibration and feedback comments, case teaching notes and errata, the instructor annexes of the cases, and the final-exam case with its blueprint form the **Educator Edition**. It is not in this repository: it is supplied to institutions that hold the CyberRiskGuardian Educational Licence, so that answers stay useful from one term to the next.

## Who may use this

- **Students** use CyberRiskGuardian and these materials free of charge for their own coursework.
- **Instructors** may adapt and reuse the materials for non-commercial teaching, with attribution.
- **Colleges, CEGEPs and universities, as institutions,** need the CyberRiskGuardian **Educational Licence** to deliver a course built on CRG: significantly reduced price, installation, customization and the Educator Edition included.
- **Non-profit organizations** use CyberRiskGuardian free of charge to assess their own risk.

## Start here

1. Instructors: read `01_Instructor_Guide`, sections 2 (the method in one page) and 5 (integration models).
2. Choose a tool track: Claude plugin, desktop application, or Excel model.
3. Run Lab 0 yourself and confirm the engine check **84,491 / 41,104**.
4. Use MediBec for the labs; assign a different case for graded work.
