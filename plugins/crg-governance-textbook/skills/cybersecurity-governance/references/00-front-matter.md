**Revision Note**

This October 2026 release (Version 2.51) completes the third edition. It extends the structural, editorial, and standards revision of the early chapters to the whole book, adds a full references section, and incorporates a complete technical and editorial review, while preserving the book's managerial and instructional focus.

**Principal changes**

• Rewrote Chapters 11 to 21 to the standard of the revised early chapters: deeper coverage, subsections, key-message callouts, illustrative scenarios, and questions managers and boards should ask; tightened Chapter 10.

• Closed the gap in chapter numbering: former Chapters 19 to 22 are now Chapters 18 to 21, and all cross-references were updated.

• Added 34 new infographics, repaired figure captions and numbering, and regenerated the tables of contents and figures and the acronym table.

• Reviewed every chapter for accuracy, currency, consistency, and tone; corrected errors in standards, laws, and figures, and updated the book to the October 2026 standards and regulatory baseline.

• Added a References section (APA 7th edition) covering the standards, laws, guidance, reports, and publications the text relies on, and a Bibliography of further reading.

• Screened the text for unattributed reuse of source material; quotations and definitions from standards and laws are now marked and attributed.

• Added post-quantum cryptography, agentic AI, exploitation-signal (EPSS, KEV, SSVC), and Canadian regulatory coverage.

• Aligned Chapters 4 to 6 and 17 with the CyberRiskGuardian method and tools: CyberRiskGuardian formulas, scenario process, tolerance bands, budget calibration, and evidence markers; added a complete CVSS v4.0 section with scoring of non-technical vulnerabilities by equivalency, and a companion appendix on using CyberRiskGuardian with this book.

• Added illustrative scenarios and questions for managers and boards to Chapters 2 to 9, and harmonized heading style.

• Added learning outcomes, a chapter summary with key terms, and review questions (five essay and twenty multiple-choice) to every chapter, with an answer key appendix; companion slide decks and an instructor resource guide are available separately.
